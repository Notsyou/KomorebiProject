About Komorebi

木漏れ日 — the Japanese concept of sunlight filtering through leaves. We believe travel should feel exactly like that.

旅

Our Philosophy

Komorebi Maps was built for travellers who move slowly, look deeply, and remember for a lifetime. We reject the algorithmic tour list. Every location in our database has been visited, vetted, and written about by people who love Japan.

🗾

Five Cities, Infinite Depth

We focus on Tokyo, Osaka, Nagoya, Okinawa, and Sapporo — not because Japan ends there, but because we believe in mastery over breadth. Detailed maps, real reviews, honest itineraries.

♡

Built for Bookmarkers

Your saved locations sync across devices the moment you sign in. Build a personal atlas of Japan — curated by you, remembered forever.

🤝

Work With Us

We partner with local guides, boutique hotels, and cultural organisations to keep our recommendations independent and our tours extraordinary. Reach us at hello@komorebi.jp
